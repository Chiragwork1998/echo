1. The agreement

By using ECHO you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use ECHO. Our privacy policy explains how we handle your data, and is part of how ECHO works.

2. Who can use ECHO

You must be 18 or over to use ECHO. By using it, you confirm that you are.

3. What ECHO is, and what it is not

ECHO is wellness software for a short daily practice of breathing, speaking or writing, and reflecting. It is not therapy, counselling, medical care, diagnosis or emergency care, and it does not replace them. The breathing practices are for when you feel a certain way, and do not treat any condition. If you have a concern about your health, please talk to a qualified professional.

If you feel dizzy or unwell during a breathing practice, stop and breathe normally. If you have a medical condition, or are pregnant, ask your doctor whether the breathing practices are right for you.

If you might harm yourself or someone else, or you are in danger, call your local emergency number now. Free helplines are listed in ECHO, under You, and on our support page.

4. Your content

Your entries, your recordings and the words made from them are yours. We claim no ownership of them. ECHO keeps them on your phone. When you choose a feature that needs it, such as turning your voice into words or reading your patterns, you allow us and our providers to process that content only to give you that feature, as our privacy policy describes. Nobody uses your content to train models.

ECHO has no cloud copy of your journal. If your phone is lost or reset, or you uninstall ECHO, your journal cannot be recovered unless you exported it first. Export is in You, under Your data.

5. Words and cards made by models

Turning your voice into words, Tidy my words and pattern cards use models run by OpenAI. Words can be misheard, and a card can misread what you meant. Read them as reflections and drafts, not as facts about you. You can report any pattern card from the card itself, and delete cards whenever you like.

The subscription. ECHO Plus is an optional subscription, sold and billed by Google Play. It comes as a yearly plan or a monthly plan. The price, and any tax, is shown in Google Play before you buy, and can differ by country.

Automatic renewal. ECHO Plus renews automatically at the end of each year or month, depending on your plan, and Google Play charges the payment method on your Google account at each renewal, until you cancel.

The free trial. The yearly plan may start with a 7 day free trial, when Google Play offers you one. You won’t be charged until the trial ends, and ECHO will remind you two days before it does. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged. A trial is for people who have not had ECHO Plus before, as Google Play decides.

Cancelling. Cancel anytime in the Google Play app: tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then ECHO. Uninstalling ECHO, or using Delete everything, does not cancel a subscription. When you cancel, ECHO Plus stays until the end of the time you have paid for, and then ECHO returns to the free plan. Your journal is not touched.

Refunds. Payments and refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies. If something went wrong, write to us at {{support_email}} and we will help where we can.

Price changes. If the price of ECHO Plus changes, Google Play will tell you before the new price applies to you, and you can cancel.

Restoring. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open ECHO Plus in You and choose Restore purchases, signed in to Google Play with the same account.

Allowances. The free plan includes 60 minutes a month of voice into words. ECHO Plus includes unlimited voice into words, within fair use of 20 hours a month. When an allowance is used up, your recordings are still kept, encrypted on your phone, and can become words later.
