Common questions

My voice didn’t become words

Your recording is safe, encrypted on your phone. When you have a connection, return to the echo and choose Turn my voice into words.

If you have used this month’s 60 free minutes, the recording waits until next month, or until you start ECHO Plus. If you chose Keep my voice only, turn on Turn my voice into words in You.

Cancel or change ECHO Plus

Open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then ECHO. Uninstalling ECHO does not cancel it. After you cancel, Plus stays until the end of the time you paid for.

Restore ECHO Plus on a new phone

Install ECHO, make sure Google Play is signed in to the account you subscribed with, then open ECHO Plus in You and choose Restore purchases.

Move my journal to a new phone

ECHO keeps your journal only on your phone, with no cloud copy. Before you change phones, export it from You, under Your data, and keep the file somewhere safe. It holds every entry as text and every recording as audio.

ECHO 1.0 cannot read an export back in, so a new phone starts a new journal.

Reminders don’t arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for ECHO in your phone’s settings, and that battery saving is not holding ECHO back. Reminders are set on your phone, so they arrive without a connection.

Delete everything

In You, under Your data, choose Delete all data, then Delete everything. Every entry, recording and setting is removed from your phone, and this cannot be undone. It does not cancel ECHO Plus; cancel that in Google Play.

To ask us to delete the few records held off your phone, write to us with your Google Play order number if you bought ECHO Plus.

Report a pattern card

Choose Report this card on the card itself. ECHO sends us the reason you chose, never the card or your words. You can also delete any card in Patterns.

Leave early access

Open the early access link again and choose to leave the test. Export your journal first if you want to keep it, then uninstall ECHO.

If you are struggling

ECHO isn’t a crisis service and can’t take the place of one. If you might act on thoughts of ending your life or hurting yourself, or you are in danger, call your local emergency number now. You don’t have to explain it well. You only have to reach.

Someone you can talk to now

  • India: Tele MANAS, 14416. Government of India. Free, day and night, in many languages.
  • United States: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text 988. Free, day and night.
  • Canada: 988 Suicide Crisis Helpline, call or text 988, day and night.
  • United Kingdom and Ireland: Samaritans, 116 123. Free to call, day and night.
  • Australia: Lifeline, 13 11 14. Call any time, or text 0477 13 11 14.
  • New Zealand: Need to talk? Call or text 1737, free, day and night.
  • Everywhere else: Find a Helpline lists free helplines by country.

Numbers last checked on 6 November 2026.
