# Google Play store listing

Ready to paste into Play Console, Grow users, Store presence, Main store listing. Every line follows
`docs/PRODUCT_V1.md`, the copy rules in `docs/DESIGN_QUALITY.md` (no banned words, no hyphens or dashes
in copy, no exclamation marks) and Play's metadata policy (no "best", "#1", "free" or calls to action
in the title, short description or graphics; no testimonials; no prices; no third party names used to
attract search). Character counts were checked by script on 8 November 2026.

Facts the owner supplies are marked `{{like_this}}`.

## App name (30 characters at most)

```
ECHO: Voice Journal & Breathe
```

29 characters. The app's own name on the phone stays ECHO; the store title adds the two things people
search for.

## Short description (80 characters at most)

```
A private voice journal and breathing for what you feel. Encrypted on your phone
```

80 characters. Play's guidance puts full stops only between sentences, so there is none at the end.

## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

```
ECHO is a private daily practice for the mind: voice journaling with breathing practices. Breathe for what you are feeling, speak for seven minutes, and see what keeps returning, in your own words. Your journal is encrypted on your phone, and there is no account to make.

Breathe for what you feel
ECHO asks one question: what are you feeling? Then it gives you a short practice made for that feeling, a breath that shifts your body and one move for your mind. Close your eyes. Sound and a calm voice lead the way, and the screen dims almost to black.

• Anxious: Settle, two breaths in and one long breath out (3 min)
• Panicky: Ground, slow breaths out while your eyes and feet find where you are (3 min)
• Hurting: Soothe, a hand on your heart and three kind sentences to yourself (4 min)
• Wanting to feel grateful: Heart, slow breathing and a moment of gratitude felt again (5 min)
• Angry: Cool, a long breath out with a quiet hum (3 min)
• Overwhelmed: One thing, box breathing and a quiet room inside you (4 min)
• Can't sleep: Sleep, in for 4, hold for 7, out for 8, then the screen goes dark (10 min)
• Low or flat: Lift, fuller breaths and one small thing that is still okay (3 min)
• Before something hard: Ready, the feeling of a time it went well (4 min)
• A craving or an urge: Ride the wave, watching an urge rise and fall without acting (3 min)

Help for panic, anxiety and hurt is never behind a paywall. Settle uses cyclic sighing: in a 2023 Stanford study, five minutes a day of cyclic sighing lifted mood more than mindfulness meditation (Balban et al., Cell Reports Medicine).

Speak for seven minutes
Answer one honest question out loud over a living mountain sky, or write if you prefer. ECHO keeps your exact words, ums and changes of mind included, and tidies them only if you ask. Watch your words appear as you speak, or keep them hidden so it is only you and the mountains. Speak English, Hindi, or both. Hindi is written in Roman script, the way you would text it.

Begin the day as who you mean to be
Mornings ask a different question: who will you be today? Arrive gives you two minutes of slow breathing, then helps you picture one ordinary moment of the day ahead as the person you are becoming. After you speak, your own words come back to you, one phrase at a time.

See what keeps returning
After five reflections, ECHO can read your latest entries and name what keeps returning, in a few quiet cards. Every quote is checked word for word against what you said, and each card offers a practice of under two minutes and one question to sit with. Cards are reflections from your own words, never a diagnosis or advice, and you can report any card from the card itself.

Private, plainly
• Your entries, recordings and pattern cards are encrypted on your phone. No account, no cloud copy.
• Your voice and entries leave your phone only after you agree, and only to OpenAI, to become words or to be read for pattern cards. OpenAI does not train on them.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.
• Export everything, or delete everything, whenever you like.

Free and ECHO Plus
Free includes unlimited writing, 60 minutes a month of voice into words, Settle, Ground and Soothe, Heart at 3 and 5 minutes, mornings, your first pattern card reading, search, export, lock and reminders. Your recordings are always kept, even when the free minutes run out.

ECHO Plus adds unlimited voice into words (fair use of 20 hours a month), words as you speak, Tidy my words, every pattern card reading, and all ten practices at any length. The yearly plan starts with 7 days free. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

The practices and cards draw on ideas from writers, teachers and researchers, each credited by name in the app. None of them has reviewed or endorsed ECHO.

ECHO is wellness software. It is not therapy, diagnosis or emergency care. If you are struggling, ECHO lists free helplines in You.

Questions or ideas: {{support_email}}
```

## Category and tags

  not pick a tag for something ECHO does not do.

## Search and keywords: the reasoning

Play ranks a listing on its title first, then the short description, then the full description, and it
penalises repetition and keyword lists. No keyword volumes were available for this plan, so the choice
below rests on relevance to what ECHO is and on the words the audience in `docs/PRODUCT_V1.md` uses.
Measure it after launch in Play Console (Acquisition reports, search terms) and change one field at a
time with Store listing experiments.

| Term | Why | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| voice journal | The category ECHO defines itself by; voice is the default way in | Title, short, full |
| journal, journaling | The broad term people search when they start | Full (twice), short |
| breathe, breathing | Breathe is now a headline feature, and the most common entry point for someone who feels anxious right now | Title, short, full |
| private, encrypted | The audience's first objection, and a reason to choose ECHO | Short, full |
| what you feel, anxious, panicky, can't sleep, angry, overwhelmed | People search by feeling, not technique; the practice list uses their words without claiming to treat anything | Short, full |
| Hindi | India first; spoken Hindi and Hinglish are written in Roman script | Full |
| gratitude, sleep, mornings, mood | Secondary intents served by Heart, Sleep and Become | Full |

Deliberately left out:

- **Authors' and organisations' names** (Joe Dispenza, Tara Brach, Gabor Maté and others). They are
  high intent searches for this audience, but using them in store text to attract search would read as
  borrowed endorsement and risks Play's metadata policy. The app and the website credit them, with the
  note that none has reviewed or endorsed ECHO.
- **"Anxiety relief", "therapy", "AI"**: ECHO does not treat anything, is not therapy, and the copy rules
  forbid AI words. The feelings are named instead: "for when you feel anxious".
- **"Free" in the title or short description**: not allowed there by Play. The full description states
  plainly what is free.
