# Target audience and content

For Play Console, Policy, App content, Target audience and content. Before this section Play wants
three things done: the Ads declaration (No), App access instructions, and the privacy policy URL.

## Target age groups

Select **18 and over** only.

ECHO is for adults: the brief's audience is 22 to 45, the terms require users to be 18 or over, the
privacy policy says ECHO is not made for children, and the practices and pattern cards deal with
personal reflection on difficult feelings.

## Restrict Minor Access: recommended on

When 18 and over is the only age group selected, Play offers Restrict Minor Access. Once on, people
Google has determined to be under 18 cannot find, install or buy ECHO; anyone under 18 who already has
it keeps it but cannot start or renew a subscription. Recommended **on**, because it is the store
enforcing what the terms already say. It costs nothing for the intended audience.

## Could the store listing unintentionally appeal to children?

**No.** The listing uses night and day mountain photography, a serif wordmark and plain adult copy
about journaling and breathing. There are no cartoon characters, games, bright children's palettes or
child focused language.

## Ads

**No ads.** ECHO contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads (`package.json`).

## App access instructions (for Google's reviewers)

ECHO has no login, so most of it is open, but some parts need conditions a reviewer may not meet. Give
these instructions under App access, choosing "All or some functionality is restricted":

1. Onboarding takes about two minutes; any answers work.
2. **Voice into words** asks for agreement the first time; choose Allow. It needs a connection.
3. **Pattern cards** appear in Patterns after 5 reflections. Write five short entries (any text), then
   open Patterns and choose Read my patterns, then agree. Use invented text, not real personal
   content.
4. **ECHO Plus** features (the six Plus practices: Cool, One thing, Sleep, Lift, Ready and Ride the
   wave; any length; words as you speak; Tidy my words; readings after the first) need a subscription.
   The yearly plan has a 7 day free trial through Google Play. Give reviewers a way in without paying,
   for example a Play promo code for the subscription, and write it here: {{review_access}}.
5. No other credentials are needed.

## Families policy and Teacher Approved

Not applicable: ECHO does not target children.
