| D-016 | 2026-11-09 | Journaling is never interrupted. Testing on his own phone, the owner said "let me journal in peace", "i just need to write freely when i am" and "it should be smooth and flawless". (1) Live words are off in 1.0. On his phone a live take came back with no words, while the same phone's recorded take was transcribed whole, and the live path has never been proved on a real microphone. Every take is recorded to a file and made into words when it ends, and "Show words" shows them then. Live words return once proven on phones. (2) ECHO shows no note of its own before the system asks for the microphone. The system's single question comes at the first moment the person speaks, and the take begins as soon as it is allowed. (3) The screen stays on while the person journals, typing and thinking included, and is let go only after five minutes with no word or touch, so a sleeping phone never locks the journal on the person writing in it. | D-007's live words while speaking; the in-app microphone explanation before the system prompt; the screen held only during a take. |
| D-017 | 2026-11-09 | After an entry, the person sees what they wrote set out, and today's echo reflected back. (1) A kept entry is set out with paragraphs, punctuation and capitals by the voice API (`/api/format`).
