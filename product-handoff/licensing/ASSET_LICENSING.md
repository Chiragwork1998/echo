# Asset and font licensing

- Scenic plates and golden references in this package were generated specifically for this ECHO concept. Keep original generation records and review commercial-use terms for the image-generation service/account used at production time.
- Do not assume a photographed real monastery, building or landscape is a verified documentary representation; these are cinematic generated scenes.
- No third-party stock photography is included.
- **New York** and **SF Pro** are Apple system fonts and must be used under Apple’s platform terms; do not redistribute font binaries.
- Cross-platform fallbacks: **Cormorant Garamond** (SIL Open Font License) and **Inter** (SIL Open Font License). Download current files from their official repositories and preserve license text.
- SVG icons in this package are original simple geometry for ECHO and can be edited with the product.
- If native SF Symbols are substituted on iOS, follow Apple’s SF Symbols license and do not use them as cross-platform brand marks.
- ECHO name/trademark clearance is not included and must be completed before launch.
