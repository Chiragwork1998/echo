# Responsive and accessibility specification

## Device ranges

- Reference: 393 × 852 pt.
- Compact: 360–374 pt wide or <800 pt high.
- Standard: 375–414 pt wide.
- Large phone: 415–430 pt wide or >900 pt high.
- Tablet: center a 430 pt cinematic column; do not stretch typography/cards across the tablet.

## Responsive rules

- Horizontal gutter: 20 pt compact, 24 pt standard, 28 pt large.
- Background always cover; use manifest focal point. Never distort.
- CTA and tab bar max width 382 pt; remain centered.
- Compact height: reduce vertical gaps before reducing type. Display XL may step 52 → 44 pt.
- Large height: add breathing room above hero and between hero/content; keep CTA bottom anchored.
- Long localization: allow title up to three lines and make body area scroll. Do not shrink below 38 pt display or 15 pt body.
- Landscape phone: supported through scroll layout; scenic crop remains decorative.

## Text scaling

- Up to 120%: preserve cinematic composition.
- 125–200%: switch to accessible scroll layout with solid/strong glass content panels and natural-height controls.
- Never truncate primary actions or journal content.

## Screen readers

- Decorative backgrounds, moon, stars, trail lines and grain are hidden.
- Progress announces “Step 3 of 8”.
- Choice controls expose selected state and full label/subtitle.
- Mood stones read “Unsettled, selected” rather than their color/position.
- Time dial has adjustable actions and an alternate native time-picker button.

## Contrast and motion

- Body text target ≥4.5:1; large display ≥3:1.
- Add local scrim behind text when photography changes; never globally darken Day.
- Selected states use shape, stroke and label—not color alone.
- Honor Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, Bold Text and Increase Contrast.
