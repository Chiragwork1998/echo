# Shipping specification

## Proposed store positioning

- Name: **ECHO — Seven Minutes to Yourself**
- Subtitle: **A private daily reflection ritual**
- Category: Health & Fitness or Lifestyle; choose after store-keyword review.
- Description lead: “A quiet seven-minute ritual for noticing what is present, putting it into words and meeting yourself with more clarity.”
- Pricing: unresolved; no paywall belongs inside the approved onboarding.
- Content rating: proposed 12+; final questionnaire/legal review required.

## Required launch artifacts

- App icon in all iOS/Android sizes.
- Store screenshots for 6.7, 6.5 and 5.5 inch iOS plus Android phone.
- Privacy policy URL, terms URL, support URL/email.
- Data safety and App Privacy answers consistent with actual SDKs.
- Account deletion in app if accounts are supported.
- Microphone, notifications and biometrics usage strings.
- Export and deletion QA evidence.

## Suggested privacy usage copy

- Microphone: “ECHO uses the microphone only when you choose to speak an entry.”
- Notifications: “ECHO can gently remind you at the time you choose.”
- Biometrics: “Use Face ID or your device biometric to keep your journal private.”

## Not included / pending

- Final bundle/application identifiers.
- Developer account credentials.
- Final legal documents.
- Final payment products and store IDs.
- Final analytics/crash/support SDK choices.
- Final app icon and store marketing set.
